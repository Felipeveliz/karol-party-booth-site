What is Karol PhotoBooth?

Karol PhotoBooth turns your iPad into a professional photo booth for weddings, quinceañeras, birthdays and corporate events. Guests take photos, videos, GIFs and 360 captures and share them instantly by AirDrop or QR — backed up to the cloud and projected to a TV via AirPlay.

Frequently asked

How do I sign in?

Karol PhotoBooth uses Sign in with Apple. Your Apple ID is the only account you need — no password to remember, and the same login syncs your events across iPads.

Where are my photos stored?

Every capture is backed up to the cloud automatically. During early access, event media is stored in a Karol PhotoBooth-managed Google Drive workspace (your own per-user Drive sign-in is on the roadmap). See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Karol PhotoBooth → Cancel. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. 24-hour Event Passes are one-time purchases and do not renew.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro / paywall screen in the app and tap “Restore purchases”. Subscriptions restore across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Captures are kept on the iPad and upload when the network returns, so the booth keeps working even with no Wi-Fi.
